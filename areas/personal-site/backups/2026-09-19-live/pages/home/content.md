PRODUCT ENGINEER · TECHNICAL PROJECT MANAGER

Hi, I'm Lorenzo.
I build products, organize complex projects, and teach what I learn.

I studied management engineering and learned software because I wanted to turn ideas into real things. Today I work across product, technology and applied AI.

I am at my best when a problem is still unclear: I give it structure, build a first working version, and keep the reasoning behind decisions clear as the work grows.

I have always loved solving problems and building things. It comes naturally to me, and I genuinely enjoy it.
