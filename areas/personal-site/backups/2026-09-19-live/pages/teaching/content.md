High scool and ITS teaching

In order to get the funds to work on BackPic I decided to work as a teacher of Logistics and Navigation Sciences in a high school for a year. During this experience I was able to learn a lot about how to teach and how to think about today's 16-18 year olds. I also developed some tools with Excel and VBA to simplify my work and to be able to teach them the basics of Excel. I also had the opportunity to teach the project management course at the master's degree course in sustainable logistics at the ITS in Parma and in the "Digital Skills" course of the Emilia Romagna Region as a teacher of a MS Office course, focused on Excel. I also taught three courses on financial education and entrepreneurship for student students. I also taught three courses on financial education and entrepreneurship for student students.
